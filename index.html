About Our Association

Welcome to Mechanical Alumni Association

Mechanical Alumni Association of Dhaka Govt. Polytechnic Institute is a platform that connects former and present students, teachers and professionals of the Mechanical Technology family.

Our mission is to build a strong alumni network, promote professional development, support students and create opportunities through unity and cooperation.
